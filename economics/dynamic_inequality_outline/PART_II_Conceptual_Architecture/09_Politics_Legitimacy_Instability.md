# Chapter 9 — Political Influence, Legitimacy, and Instability

**Guiding question:** How can distributional inequality alter institutions and public capacity?

## Chapter deliverable

A finished chapter should make one clear argument, define every concept used later, distinguish established mechanisms from original synthesis, and end by motivating the next chapter.

## Proposed structure

### Influence

- Campaign resources, lobbying, organization, information, and agenda access.

### Policy channels

- Taxation, regulation, public investment, credit, housing, labor institutions, and social insurance.

### Legitimacy

- Perceived fairness, trust, compliance, participation, and acceptance.

### Instability

- Conflict, polarization, policy volatility, disruption, and recovery.

### State capacity

- Revenue, implementation, administration, and service maintenance.

### Conditional loop

- Inequality → exclusion → lower legitimacy → disruption/state-capacity loss → weaker services → inequality.
