# Chapter 5 — Stocks of Advantage and Disadvantage

**Guiding question:** Which conditions accumulate over time and belong in the model as stocks?

## Chapter deliverable

A finished chapter should make one clear argument, define every concept used later, distinguish established mechanisms from original synthesis, and end by motivating the next chapter.

## Proposed structure

### Assets

- Financial, housing, business, productive, and liquid assets.

### Debt

- Treat debt separately from assets; include service, default, refinancing, and deleveraging.

### Net wealth

- Define net wealth as derived.
$$
W_g(t)=A_g(t)-D_g(t)
$$

### Human capital

- Education, health, skills, experience, formation, and depreciation.

### Network capital

- Relationships, referrals, information, trust, formation, and decay.

### Political influence

- Resources, organization, access, agenda setting, accumulation, and decay.

### Institutional legitimacy

- Accumulated trust, perceived fairness, compliance, and acceptance.

### Selection criteria

- Accumulation, persistence, independent dynamics, measurability, and explanatory necessity.

## Evidence to collect

- Primary theoretical source for every major mechanism.
- Best empirical estimate for each causal relationship or parameter.
- Scope conditions and conflicting findings.

## Planned figures and tables

- One figure only when it clarifies structure, behavior, or measurement.
- One table mapping claims to evidence, variables, equations, or data when useful.

## Writing prompts

- What must the reader believe by the end of this chapter?
- Which claims are literature-backed, and which are hypotheses?
- Which terms require operational definitions?
- Which causal arrows require direct evidence?
- What assumptions or boundary conditions must be explicit?
- How does the final paragraph motivate the next chapter?
