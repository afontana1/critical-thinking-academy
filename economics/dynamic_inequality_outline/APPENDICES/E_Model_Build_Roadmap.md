# Appendix E — Staged Model Build Roadmap

## Version 0 — Accounting sandbox
Assets, debt, net wealth, income, saving; no behavioral feedback beyond accounting.

## Version 1 — Minimal cumulative model
Asset accumulation, heterogeneous initial conditions, labor income, capital income, saving, and redistribution.

## Version 2 — Credit and debt
Borrowing access, borrowing cost, productive investment, defensive borrowing, default, and asset loss.

## Version 3 — Human capital and services
Human-capital and service-quality stocks, education/health investment, and labor-income response.

## Version 4 — Intergenerational transmission
Inheritance, cohort turnover, parent-child transitions, and a mobility matrix.

## Version 5 — Networks and political feedback
Network capital, influence, policy endogeneity, and public-investment allocation.

## Version 6 — Legitimacy and instability
Conditional legitimacy erosion, state-capacity effects, and shock propagation.

## Promotion criteria

- All unit tests pass.
- Dimensional consistency is verified.
- Loop-isolation tests match expected behavior.
- Added complexity improves explanation or empirical performance.
- Parameters are identifiable or transparently constrained.
