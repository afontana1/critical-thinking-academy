# Dynamic Inequality Systems Theory — Research Monograph Skeleton

## Working thesis

> Economic inequality is a dynamic asymmetry in accumulated resources, capabilities, risks, and institutional access. It is produced by economic and political processes while simultaneously altering the opportunity sets and transition probabilities governing future outcomes.

## Intended use

This is a structured outline, not a completed manuscript. Each chapter provides a guiding question, proposed subsections, evidence prompts, and a completion checklist.

## Recommended drafting order

1. Draft Chapters 1, 3, 10, 11, and 12 to lock the argument and model boundary.
2. Draft Chapters 4–9 to justify mechanisms.
3. Draft Chapters 13–14 while implementing the mathematical model.
4. Draft Chapters 15–17 alongside pseudo-data testing and real-data calibration.
5. Draft Chapters 18–21 only after stable model results exist.
